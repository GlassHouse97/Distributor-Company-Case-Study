# Visualizations

These are the final charts used by the written answers and Word report. They contain no customer names or source customer numbers.

| File | Question |
| --- | --- |
| `01_segment_profitability.png` | Segment revenue share compared with gross-profit share |
| `02_customer_concentration.png` | Customer cumulative revenue concentration |
| `03_retention_risk.png` | Lifecycle mix and historical revenue baseline at risk |
| `04_revenue_margin_trends.svg` | Browser-friendly annual revenue and gross-margin trend |
| `04_revenue_margin_trends.png` | Word-report version of the annual revenue and margin trend |

Questions 1-4 regenerate their main charts with `scripts/analyze_q1.py` through `scripts/analyze_q4.py`. The Word-report PNG for Question 4 is regenerated by `scripts/build_business_report.py`.
